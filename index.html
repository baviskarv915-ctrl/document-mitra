About Aadhaar

Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identity number issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) to all residents of India.

How to Apply

  • Visit your nearest Aadhaar enrollment center
  • Bring required documents (birth certificate, address proof)
  • Complete biometric registration
  • Receive Aadhaar slip via SMS

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate
  • Address proof (utility bill, passport)
  • Photo ID (passport, driving license)

Useful Links

  • UIDAI Official Website
  • Download e-Aadhaar
  • MyAadhaar Portal

About PAN

Permanent Account Number (PAN) is a 10-character alphanumeric identifier issued by the Indian Income Tax Department.

How to Apply

  • Visit the TIN-NSDL official website
  • Fill PAN application form
  • Submit documents online
  • Pay application fee
  • Receive PAN via post

Documents Required

  • Proof of identity (Aadhaar, passport)
  • Proof of address (electricity bill)
  • Date of birth proof

Useful Links

  • TIN-NSDL Portal
  • e-PAN Services
  • Income Tax Portal

About Income Certificate

Income certificate is an official document issued by the district administration that proves your annual income. For Maharashtra applications, use the Aaple Sarkar portal.

How to Apply

  • Visit Maharashtra Aaple Sarkar portal
  • Fill the income certificate application form
  • Submit required documents
  • Pay the prescribed fee
  • Collect the certificate within 7-15 days

Documents Required

  • Aadhar card or voter ID
  • Address proof
  • Last 3 years income tax returns (if applicable)
  • Bank statements

Useful Links

  • Maharashtra Aaple Sarkar Portal

About Caste Certificate

Caste certificate is issued by the district administration to individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, or Other Backward Classes. For Maharashtra applications, use the Aaple Sarkar portal.

How to Apply

  • Visit Maharashtra Aaple Sarkar portal
  • Provide community/caste proof documents
  • Complete verification process
  • Receive certificate after approval

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate
  • Voter ID or Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Caste/community proof (family certificate)
  • Affidavit

Useful Links

  • Maharashtra Aaple Sarkar Portal

About Domicile/Residence Certificate

Domicile certificate proves that you are a resident of a particular state and is required for state-specific benefits and admissions. For Maharashtra applications, use the Aaple Sarkar portal.

How to Apply

  • Visit Maharashtra Aaple Sarkar portal
  • Fill domicile certificate application form
  • Submit residence proof documents
  • Pay application fee
  • Receive certificate within 7-15 days

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate or passport
  • Address proof (utility bill, property tax receipt)
  • Aadhaar card or voter ID
  • School/college certificates (if applicable)

Useful Links

  • Maharashtra Aaple Sarkar Portal
